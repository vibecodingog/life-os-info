Life OS Personal · Privacy · Terms

Privacy policy

Effective September 15, 2026. This policy covers the Life OS Personal Google Calendar integration.

Access and use

With the owner's Google authorization, the integration accesses calendar metadata and event information, including names, dates, descriptions, locations, participants, recurrence, and meeting links. It uses this information to display the owner's schedule and carry out explicitly requested calendar operations. Event changes and invitations are sent to Google and intended participants only as directed by the owner.

Storage and security

The private dashboard and its authorization credentials are maintained on owner-controlled equipment. Calendar responses are processed locally to render the dashboard. Local working records, backups, or owner-requested task records may be retained until removed by the owner. They are not published on this website or placed in its public repository. Access is restricted to the owner's authorized devices and tools.

Sharing

Calendar data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train general-purpose AI models. Routine calendar display does not require an AI service. If the owner explicitly requests an assistant task involving calendar information, the selected information may be processed by the owner's configured assistant provider for that task. The integration does not independently publish or broadly share calendar data.

Life OS Personal's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Control and deletion

The owner can revoke access in their Google Account connections settings. Revocation stops future authorized API access but does not automatically erase retained local records or backups; those are managed separately by the owner. Deleting local copies does not delete the source Google Calendar events unless the owner explicitly requests that action.

This informational website

This site contains static pages hosted by GitHub Pages. It has no sign-in, forms, analytics scripts, advertising, or access to the private dashboard. GitHub may process ordinary hosting request information under its own privacy statement.

Contact and changes

For non-sensitive project questions, contact the maintainer through the project issue tracker. Never post calendar contents or credentials there. Material changes to the integration's data use require an updated policy and the owner's consent.
