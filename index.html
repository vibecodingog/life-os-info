Life OS Personal

A private, local-first personal organizer. The Google Calendar integration displays the account owner's calendars and events and supports owner-requested event creation, updates, deletion, and meeting links.

This integration is for the owner's personal use, not a public sign-up service. Google authorization is handled directly by Google. This website is informational only: it does not host the dashboard, collect Google credentials, or display calendar or health records.

The dashboard runs on the owner's own equipment and is accessed through a private network. Google remains the calendar source of truth.
